## Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

### Approach

I used a simple nested-loop approach to check every pair of numbers. When a pair adds up to the target, its two indices are returned.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n²)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes

The solution handles duplicate values and returns the first matching pair found.
